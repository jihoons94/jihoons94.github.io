Oil Desalination Plant

Ahvaz Central Oil Desalination Plant Project

The oil desalination plant located in Ahvaz, Iran, processes high-salt crude oil produced from the Ahvaz oil field. The facility separates oil and water to ensure industrial wastewater meets environmental standards. DAEHWA supplied oil-water separation pumps to this facility, directly contributing to the operation of Iran's key oil facilities.

Global

Reverse Osmosis Seawater Desalination Plant

Jeddah SWRO (Reverse Osmosis Seawater Desalination)-3 Plant Project

The reverse osmosis seawater desalination - 3 plant located in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia consists of intake, pretreatment, RO, and post-treatment processes, and includes wastewater treatment facilities that process wastewater generated from pretreatment and reverse osmosis processes in the desalination treatment process. DAEHWA delivered volute pumps for sodium bisulfite transfer suitable for the site's process through professional solutions tailored to the material and specifications required for the facility.

Global

Reverse Osmosis Seawater Desalination Plant

Shuaibah RO (Reverse Osmosis Seawater Desalination) Plant Project

The reverse osmosis seawater desalination plant located in Shuaibah, Kuwait produces approximately 270,000 tons of desalinated water per day, supplying drinking water to 900,000 people. DAEHWA delivered volute pumps for caustic soda transfer suitable for the site's process through professional solutions tailored to the material and specifications required for the facility.

Global

Steel Mill CAL (Continuous Annealing Line)

POSCO India Maharashtra Steel CAL Line

POSCO Maharashtra Steel, located in India, provides high-quality galvanized and galvanized heat-treated steel plates applicable to various industries including construction, home appliances, and automotive industries. Among them, the CAL line of this plant is used to modify the grain structure of steel through heat treatment and change properties including hardness and strength. DAEHWA contributed to the CAL line process of POSCO Maharashtra Steel in India by supplying sump pumps and other equipment to the facility.
